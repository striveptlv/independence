A functional digital IADL activity for practicing real-world flight booking skills: choosing airports, comparing fares and schedules, following task directions, managing budgets and baggage fees, tracking passenger preferences, reviewing errors, and completing a structured mock booking sequence with clinical support.

Choose A Booking Task

Using This With A Patient

Use STRIVE Airlines as a guided community-access simulation. Match the task and support level to the patient, then review performance together.

1. Choose The Task

Select the booking task that matches the patient’s attention, language, visual scanning, memory, math, and executive-function needs.

2. Preview The Goal

Read the task directions together and identify the cities, dates, budget, passenger needs, or special rules before starting.

3. Search Flights

Have the patient enter airports and dates, use location support if helpful, and scan the complete search form before continuing.

4. Compare Options

Encourage systematic left-to-right scanning, price comparison, layover math, cancellation checks, and budget planning.

5. Review Booking

Verify passenger details, preferences, baggage fees, dates, flight times, and total cost before completing the simulation.

6. Debrief Together

Discuss errors, cueing, strategies, and carryover needs for real travel planning and community independence.
